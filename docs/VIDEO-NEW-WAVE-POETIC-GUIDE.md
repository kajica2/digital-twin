# New Wave Poetic — V1 Video Guide (7 frames)

> Stills + video prompts for a 7-scene music video in the twin's own
> world: poetic songwriting imagery staged in new wave video language.
> Still images live in
> `assets/mural-prompts/new-wave-poetic-7frames.md` (no `--motion`
> flags there); this doc carries the video prompts, the pairing rules,
> and the motion cheat-sheet. Each frame is tone-mapped to
> `docs/PERSONALITY-TONE-GUIDE.md`.

## Frame 1 — The Lyric Page *(Deep House — momentum)*

**Video:** slow push-in on a handwritten lyric page, ink drying as the camera nears, the pen lifting off the final word, the blind-striped light shifting once as a shadow passes the window. Atmosphere: private, midnight, deliberate. Style: macro, venetian blinds light, neon-blue accent, 1980s new wave music video, 50mm, shallow DOF. `--ar 16:9 --motion low`

*Tone map:* the writing is the groove; the pen lifts like a downbeat.

---

## Frame 2 — The Vocalist / Blinds *(Soundhealing — breath)*

**Video:** locked-off close-up, the vocalist draws breath, the shadow stripes stay fixed while the smoke drifts, eyes stay closed through the phrase. One gesture, one emotion. Atmosphere: tender, private, luminous. Style: moonlight through blinds, single cyan gel, smoke, Kodak Portra grain pushed cool. `--ar 16:9 --motion low`

*Tone map:* the parted mouth is a held breath — stillness with intent. (Soundhealing's "silence is a note".)

---

## Frame 3 — The Synth Hover *(Cyberpunk/HK — electricity)*

**Video:** slow lateral drift along the synth panel, the hovering finger strikes the key, LEDs catch, the rain-streaked window reflection moves with the camera. Atmosphere: charged, late-night, one touch away. Style: analog synth close-up, purple-cyan glow, black gloss, anamorphic. `--ar 16:9 --motion medium`

*Tone map:* the hover is tension; the strike is the city idling into pulse.

---

## Frame 4 — The Platform *(Cyberpunk/HK × Deep House)*

**Video:** wide locked shot, the neon flickers, the lone figure at the far end turns and walks toward camera, the wet concrete holding the reflection the whole way. Atmosphere: lonely, monumental, 4 a.m. Style: teal and copper, neon reflection, anamorphic, film grain. `--ar 16:9 --motion low`

*Tone map:* the empty platform is infrastructure — the figure is the only signal, exactly like the green light on the far shore.

---

## Frame 5 — The Brass Corridor *(Soundhealing × Identity)*

**Video:** slow tracking down the corridor toward the silhouette, the trumpet lifts into the sodium shaft, the note's start shakes the smoke. Atmosphere: cold, reverent, a held note. Style: new wave noir, single sodium key, 35mm, deep blacks. `--ar 16:9 --motion low`

*Tone map:* the bell catching light is a threshold — brass as the loudest quiet thing.

---

## Frame 6 — The Singing Bowl *(Soundhealing — still)*

**Video:** slow orbit around the bowl, the vapor thread curls upward and becomes faint light, the room tilts a degree as the camera completes the orbit. Atmosphere: uncanny, soft, dreamlike. Style: chiaroscuro, warm key, painterly grain, cinematic widescreen. `--ar 16:9 --motion medium`

*Tone map:* the bowl is furniture — something you can put a tired person on; the orbit is the room agreeing.

---

## Frame 7 — The Typewriter End-Card *(Deep House last line)*

**Video:** locked-off, the typewriter arm strikes the final letter, the scanlines pulse once, the light blooms then dims — fade to the closing title. Atmosphere: final, certain, quiet after the work. Style: 1980s end-card, magenta-blue, CRT scanlines, soft bloom. `--ar 16:9 --motion low`

*Tone map:* the coda — a soft landing with a pulse; the last keystroke is the downbeat fading.

## Pairing rules (this pack)

1. **The still holds the start of the motion** — the wet ink, the parted mouth, the hovering finger, the flicker-blur, the note not yet struck, the vapor thread, the raised typewriter arm. Complete actions read as steady state to V1.
2. **Composition anticipates direction.** The platform figure stands center-far because the push-in owns the corridor. The pen rests right where the push-in will land.
3. **Style language must match still ↔ video.** "1980s new wave music video" appears in both sides of every pair; "venetian blinds" in frame 2's still and its video prompt say the same thing.
4. **Aspect ratios must match** — this set is **16:9 everywhere**, held identically in the still and the video prompt.

## Motion cheat-sheet

| Setting | Frames |
|---|---|
| `low` | 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 — locked or single-movement frames |
| `medium` | 3, 6 — a camera move with the subject's single action |

New-wave specifics: one gel color + one light geometry (blinds/shaft) is a complete style anchor; the scanlines and end-card bloom belong only to frame 7 — don't stack them elsewhere or the look muddies.

## V1 structure notes (as established)

- `Camera:` first word commits the motion type.
- One film stock OR one lens — not three references.
- One person, one object, one place per frame.
- Avoid multi-character choreography and continuity-hinged events.
