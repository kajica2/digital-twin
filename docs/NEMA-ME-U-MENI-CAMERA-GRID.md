# Nema Me U Meni — the camera grid

> 12 cinematic shots · Twelve beats, twelve cameras · Belgrade · Analog noir

**Concept.** The same song, the same man, the same twelve moments — but each moment seen from a different place a camera can stand. The beats come from the master template; the cameras are the grid. Together they teach where a camera can be, one beat at a time.

**Source.** The master template `lib/nema-me-u-meni.json` — same beats, same DNA, same grade, new cameras.

**Visual DNA.** 1970s European arthouse cinema, faded Kodak 500T, tobacco browns, dirty teal, nicotine yellow, winter gray, soft halation, analog grain, practical light, natural imperfections, unposed documentary realism, 35mm anamorphic

**Flags.** `--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 75 --chaos 8 --hd`

**Identity clause.** no red coat and no denim jacket and no leather jacket and no necklace, shaved very short haircut

**Render plain.** No `--edit`, no `--cref` — measured, the attachment
decides the distance and the camera clause would be disobeyed.
