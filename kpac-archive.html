Three eras of kpac.org

1999 to 2008

Artists' collective

KPAC ran as a working artists' collective. Member pages, projects, and community notices. Real content, varied voice, the kind of site you only get when people who care are running it.

38 snapshots
2009 to 2016 · 2025 to present

Minimal placeholder

The collective had stopped updating. The site was reduced to near-empty pages, sometimes just a single line. The domain still resolved, but the people were gone.

3 snapshots
2017 to 2024

Parked domain

The domain had expired and was being sold by a parking service. The "content" is dense JavaScript rendering the price tag. Visible text is mostly "this website is for sale."
