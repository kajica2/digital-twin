# Morning Haunt — film grain, warm tones, nostalgic drift

> Aesthetic: songs that feel like memories — indie folk, ambient,
> singer-songwriter, lo-fi hip-hop. 80–110 BPM. Morning light through
> net curtains, the hour after a party when the house is still asleep,
> objects holding the warmth of whoever last touched them.
>
> Film grain is load-bearing. Warm tones are not orange — they are
> the colour of light at 9am in an apartment you've lived in for years.
> Nostalgic drift means: the image should feel like it remembers something
> you cannot quite name.
>
> Subject first. No text in frame. --ar 16:9 on every bullet.
