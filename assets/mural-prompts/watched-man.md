# The Watched Man — 12 frames (16:9)

> 12 cinematic frames · A man under observation · Belgrade · Analog noir
>
> Source: the artist's own storyboard — "A man moves through an ordinary day while never quite escaping notice. An institutional corridor at dawn, an observation room, a stairwell where his reflection lags, a crossing where the city smears past him, a café he does not really occupy, an apartment where the instrument stays on the chair, and a rooftop where the sky turns and he is gone. Nothing supernatural happens. The strangeness is in the framing: he is watched, and the watching is in the frame — high angles, empty foregrounds, one light source, and long holds."
>
> One consistent male figure throughout all twelve frames. He does not need to dominate the composition — sometimes he is a silhouette, a reflection, or a small figure in a large institutional space. The moodboard is the house one; the frames are about where the camera is relative to him.
>
> Visual DNA (appended to every frame): 1970s European arthouse cinema, faded Kodak 500T, tobacco browns, dirty teal, nicotine yellow, winter gray, soft halation, analog grain, practical light, natural imperfections, unposed documentary realism, 35mm anamorphic
