> collection of curated `.swr-set` files; this pack gives every preset a
> distinct cover plate.
>
> Prompt style note: subject first, shared style clause at the tail. The
> acceptance matcher keys on the prompt's opening characters, so every
> opening here is deliberately different — six plates that collapse into one
> are worse than no plates.
>
> No text. Diffusion models paint letter-shaped marks rather than composing
> glyphs, so any lettering in the image is wrong (see TD-007). Titles are set
> in the layout, not generated.

> Submit: copy into `prompts-inbox/` — the watcher renders one generation
> per prompt.
