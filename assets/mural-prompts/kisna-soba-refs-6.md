style-note: >
  The consistency system for the series, in the order it has to happen:

  1. the STYLE sheet (the first bullet) is rendered on its own; its own
     output URL becomes STYLE_REF and is fed to every sheet below as
     `--sref`;
  2. the three character sheets and the group sheet are rendered with
     that `--sref`;
  3. every episode frame then carries `--cref <sheet URL> --cw 100` for
     each character in it, plus the same `--sref` and the same negative
     tail.

  The negative tail is the same in every prompt on purpose: the frames
  and the sheets must live in the same world.
