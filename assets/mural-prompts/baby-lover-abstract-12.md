> 01 · VERSE — it's so long.

- A single rope of white light dragged horizontally across a black frame by a long exposure, halation bleeding off both edges, the blurred smear of a standing figure at the far left, overexposed film, coarse grain, no readable shapes, muted palette, no text

> 02 · VERSE — been so far and wide.

- A flat horizon of drifting dust and haze splitting the frame into a peach band and a grey band, an out-of-focus arm entering from the bottom edge, no landmarks, empty negative space, fine grain, muted palette, no text

> 03 · VERSE — time like this will never be.

- Dozens of clock faces reflected in broken mirror shards laid on black velvet, the reflections repeating and misaligning, a hand's fingers resting among the shards, cold metallic highlights, macro lens, fine grain, muted palette, no text

> 04 · VERSE — and that's just the way.

- Extreme abstract texture: a shoulder and half a jaw dissolving into coarse emulsion grain at the edge of the frame, the rest is pure film surface with dust, scratches and a light leak across the top, no full face, muted palette, no text

> 05 · VERSE — moments of past, moments begin.

- Double exposure of an empty corridor and a face at thirty percent opacity, the two geometries overlapping and offset, warm light on one layer and cold on the other, ghosting at the joins, fine grain, muted palette, no text

> 06 · CHORUS — breathe in.

- A single cloud of frozen breath sculpted into a white shape against a black field, backlit, the vapour crystallising at its edges, only a chin and lower lip at the very bottom of the frame, no eyes, fine grain, muted palette, no text

> 07 · CHORUS — open your eyes right wide.

- Macro of a human iris filling the entire frame like a landscape, fibres and rings in sharp detail, one catchlight shaped like a window, the pupil at the centre, shallow depth of field, fine grain, no text

> 08 · CHORUS — make your dreams reality.

- Two hands cupped around a small sun of white light, the light blown out and bleeding halation across most of the frame, only the outlines of the fingers readable, heavy grain, muted palette, no text

> 09 · VERSE — baby lover coming my way.

- Wet asphalt abstracted into vertical streaks of red and amber light, a reflection of a silhouette with no body attached to it, the surface broken by rain, everything out of focus except the light, fine grain, no text

> 10 · VERSE — time to come and play.

- A strip of white fabric whipping through a dark frame as one long curved shape, an arm reduced to a blurred arc behind it, a flash of colour on the cloth, motion smear, heavy grain, no text

> 11 · VERSE — time to steal away.

- A door-shaped rectangle of white light standing in a black field, soft-focus edges, a thrown shadow of a hand reaching into the light from the bottom corner, nothing else in frame, fine grain, no text

> 12 · VERSE — beedle-dee together, beedle-dee the same.

- Two silhouettes merged into a single dark shape against a pale dawn, almost a graphic mark, the edges soft and grainy, immense empty sky, restrained, fine grain, no text
