  the decision that separates one take from another is not the subject,

> 01 · APPROACH A — the literal cut: the camera where a camera operator would stand.

- A man in a dark coat over a black roll-neck standing in a doorway swung wide in the middle of the night, the camera at chest height in the street facing him square on, the empty road behind him with one streetlamp burning far down it, the lit room over his shoulders, no denim jacket and no leather jacket and no necklace, shaved very short haircut, fine natural film grain, muted palette, no text

> 02 · APPROACH B — the single take: one move, no cut, the camera leaving the room.

- A man in a dark coat over a black roll-neck standing in a doorway swung wide in the middle of the night, seen from deep inside the dark room with the camera low at the threshold moving out toward him, the door frame passing close across the edges of the frame, the street and the far streetlamp opening up behind him, no denim jacket and no leather jacket and no necklace, shaved very short haircut, fine natural film grain, muted palette, no text

> 03 · APPROACH C — the graphic: the frame as a poster, the figure only a shape.

- The edge of a door swung wide at night and the light spilling out of it across wet pavement, the open doorway a hard bright vertical shape inside a black frame, a man in a dark coat over a black roll-neck only a dark shape in the opening with no face visible, high contrast, deep shadow, no denim jacket and no leather jacket and no necklace, fine natural film grain, muted palette, no text

> 04 · APPROACH D — the rhythm cut: the frame caught on the beat, hard light.

- A man in a dark coat over a black roll-neck stepping through a doorway swung wide in the middle of the night, caught at the exact instant of the beat with the room's light flaring across the wet road in front of him, the streetlamp behind him blown out, his coat still moving, no denim jacket and no leather jacket and no necklace, shaved very short haircut, fine natural film grain, muted palette, no text

> 05 · APPROACH E — the held moment: the light changes while the frame holds.

- A man in a dark coat over a black roll-neck standing in a doorway swung wide in the middle of the night, the far streetlamp burning low and the sky just beginning to lift at the horizon behind him, the light moving across his face from lamp-orange to the first cold blue of morning, no denim jacket and no leather jacket and no necklace, shaved very short haircut, fine natural film grain, muted palette, no text
