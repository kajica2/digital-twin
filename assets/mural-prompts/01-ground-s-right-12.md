  underside of a road — an excavated pit under a broken street, a flooded
  cellar, a gallery cut into the earth, a manhole shaft. The register is
  wet browns, peat, iron and old concrete with analogue grain; the light
  comes from above (an open manhole, a dirty window, a bulb on a flex)
  or from one lamp standing in the dark. The wardrobe is the dark coat
