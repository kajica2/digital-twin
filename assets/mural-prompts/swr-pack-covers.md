# SWR Image-Pack Covers — 5 prompts (16:9)

> Source: the SWR image-pack line — Neon Church (electric · club ·
> urban), Morning Haunt (nostalgic · indie · lo-fi), Brutalist Grid
> (hard geometry · monochrome), Dream Sequence (blur · haze · slow),
> System Error (glitch · chaos · RGB). One cover per pack, in pack
> order, one MJ job each (4-up grid to pick from).
>
> Unlettered — set names get typeset over these afterwards. For square
> thumbnails instead, change `--ar 16:9` to `--ar 1:1` per bullet
> before submitting.
>
> Prompt order: 1 Neon Church · 2 Morning Haunt · 3 Brutalist Grid ·
> 4 Dream Sequence · 5 System Error.

- Neon church interior at night: a dark concrete nave lit only by magenta and cyan neon tubes, a club crowd silhouetted between the pillars, haze and wet-floor reflections, urban grit, deep blacks, electric atmosphere, cinematic photograph --ar 16:9
- Morning haunt: pale sunrise light through a dusty window in an empty loft, a translucent ghosted double exposure of a figure at the glass, lo-fi film grain and soft halation, muted peach and grey palette, nostalgic and melancholic, 35mm photograph --ar 16:9
- Brutalist grid: a raw concrete housing block shot dead-on, hard geometry of shadowed windows and diagonal stairwells, extreme contrast, monochrome, cracked concrete texture, dust in the air, black and white architectural photograph --ar 16:9
- Dream sequence: a figure walking through low mist at dusk, extreme soft focus, slow-shutter motion blur, hazy pastel gradients dissolving the horizon, light bleeding across the frame, weightless and slow, ethereal photograph --ar 16:9
- System error: a fragmented face and city skyline tearing into RGB channel splits, datamosh artifacts, scanline glitch, chromatic aberration, saturated red green blue error blocks over black, aggressive electronic visual chaos --ar 16:9
