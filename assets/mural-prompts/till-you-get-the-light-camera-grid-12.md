engine-note: >
  The same lyric as the `till-you-get-the-light` set, in the surreal /
  vintage / grotesque register, and with ONE VARIABLE: every frame is a
  different camera. Twelve beats, twelve camera positions — wide, low,
  high, rolled, macro, over-the-shoulder, profile, top-down, long lens,
  panning, from behind, front-on. The frames are the classic camera grid
  so the set teaches where a camera can be, one beat at a time.
  The suffix is the artist's, byte-for-byte on every bullet, so the only
  variables are the moment and the camera. It owns `--ar`, `--raw`,
  `--sref`, `--profile`, `--stylize` and `--chaos`, so the config's own
  defaults are stripped rather than appended alongside (two `--s` flags
  is the duplicated-flag class, and MJ honours the LAST one).
  Plain generation — no identity attachment: the suffix carries no
  `--edit`, and these frames are about the camera, not about a face.
  The camera clause comes BEFORE the style words, so the camera reads as
  the instruction and the style as the grade on top of it.
