> 9 case stills (S-01…S-09) + 10 thesis stills (T-01…T-10) in the
> **PLATE** house look: provenance · one practical + one sourceless fill ·
> exactly one deliberate discontinuity · depth of field as the

> **These are the Midjourney-adapted prompts.** The full prose is the
> source of truth in the firing-21 brief

> prose condensed for Midjourney, keeping every element the **plate
> check** measures: the subject, the ONE deliberate discontinuity, the
> one practical plus one sourceless fill, the one clipped-to-white
> region, the depth-of-field register and the one accent hue.
>
> **Why condensed, and why this length** — measured 2026-10-22 against
> the live web backend:
>
>   * Midjourney REWRITES a prompt over ~1000 characters. The brief's
>     1,800-character versions came back compressed ("A wide, static
>     shot at the mouth of a tenement stoop" → "Wide static shot,
>     tenement stoop mouth"), losing exactly the measurable details the
>     plate check asks for. 922 characters came back verbatim; 1,006 did
>     not. Every prompt here is under 900 with its flags, so nothing is
>     rewritten.
>   * The account's personalization (profile #8 + four moodboards) is
>     applied to every generation whatever the prompt says, and no flag
>     can turn it off. `lib/mj-web.js --no-personalize` clears it.
>
> Per the brief's own generator notes: `--style raw` is essential (the
> house look is documentary and MJ's default will ornate it). Aspect per
> still: 16:9, except **S-02** and **T-04** (2.39:1 → `--ar 21:9`) and
> **S-03** (9:16).
