# Source Frames — 10-Day Video (mixed AR)

> Source: 10-scene image-to-video storyboard (V1 source frames).
> One frame per day. `Tune` notes live in blockquotes — the watcher
> ignores them, the human reads them; they encode what V1 needs from
> the still (motion seeds, gaze direction, empty space to animate
> into).

- Extreme close-up of a single wet fern frond, water droplets clinging to the tips, backlit by soft overcast light, one ant visible at the lower-left edge of frame just entering, shallow depth of field, background dissolved into cool green --ar 16:9 --v 7 --style raw
  > Tune: keep the ant small and slightly off-center-left — the video's lateral tracking reads better when there's "room" for the motion to push into.
- Wide shot of a brutalist concrete corridor at dusk, parallel lines converging to a distant doorway, one small figure walking toward camera in the deep background, hard sidelight from a clerestory window, dust visible in the air, anamorphic horizontal flare, desaturated palette, cool blues, single warm highlight on the concrete --ar 16:9 --v 7
  > Tune: the figure needs to be small enough that V1 has "space" to render the push-in. Don't make the figure the dominant subject of the still — let the architecture lead.
- Medium close-up of a person in their 40s at a kitchen table, late afternoon window light raking across the face, coffee cup held mid-lift in one hand, eyes looking past camera toward the window, slight tiredness in the eyes, no smile, Kodak Portra 400 palette, warm tungsten plus cool daylight mix --ar 4:3 --v 7 --style raw
  > Tune: the "look past camera" beat must already be in the still — V1 animates from the gaze, not toward it. If they're looking at the camera in the still, the video will likely keep them looking at camera the whole time.
- Tight close-up of two hands resting on the corner of a vintage film poster, fingers just touching the peeling backing, paper fibers and small air bubbles visible under the lifted edge, warm tungsten sidelight from a window, dust drifting through the light beam, 16mm grain, 60mm lens, shallow depth of field --ar 16:9 --v 7
  > Tune: hands should be at the moment of "just about to peel," not mid-peel. The video's motion is the peeling itself — if the still is already mid-peel, V1 often doesn't know what direction to continue.
- Low-angle wide shot across a vast salt flat at golden hour, shallow water mirroring a sky of scattered cumulus, a single dark horse walking left-to-right in the mid-distance small against the horizon, warm amber light, slight haze, anamorphic horizontal compression, 35mm cinematic palette --ar 21:9 --v 7
  > Tune: the horse should already be mid-stride in the still, legs slightly apart. Standing-still horse in the still often gets "frozen" in the video — V1 needs a starting motion pose to read "this thing is walking."
- A single porcelain teacup with a chipped rim, floating at chest height in the middle of a dark wood-panelled room, soft chiaroscuro lighting, a single warm key from upper-left, a faint wisp of steam rising from the cup dissolving into shadow, painterly texture, soft lens diffusion, square frame --ar 1:1 --v 7
  > Tune: the steam should be barely there — a single wisp, not a column. V1 is more willing to "grow" the steam into the birds when the still leaves room for it. Heavy steam in the still = the video doesn't know what to do with it.
- Handheld eye-level shot of a wet market stall at dawn, a vendor's hands arranging oranges in a wooden crate in the foreground slightly motion-blurred, soft steam from a noodle cart drifting left across the mid-ground, available fluorescent plus dawn light mix, slightly desaturated, 16mm newsreel grain, 28mm wide lens --ar 16:9 --v 7 --style raw
  > Tune: hands-blur is the key — V1 preserves "this is in motion" when the still has visible motion blur. If the still is sharp, V1 often snaps everything to stillness before re-animating, which kills the documentary feel.
- Locked-off wide shot of a single-story motel parking lot at night, a buzzing neon "VACANCY" sign glowing red-blue against a stucco wall, empty lot in the foreground, deep blacks, one lamppost casting a hard yellow pool of light, sodium-vapor warm/cool color contrast, anamorphic, 2.39:1 --ar 2.39:1 --v 7
  > Tune: the still must be empty of cars. The car is the motion. If a car is already parked in the still, V1 either ignores the incoming vehicle or composites awkwardly. The sign should be slightly flickering (slight blur on the neon) — gives V1 permission to keep flickering in the video.
- Low-angle fisheye shot of a skateboarder mid-ollie in a sunlit concrete underpass, board angled, wheels off the ground, one arm out for balance, sun flare from upper-right, concrete pillars blurring in the periphery, Super 8 transferred to video palette, oversaturated warm tones, 16mm grain, heavy fisheye distortion --ar 16:9 --v 7
  > Tune: the ollie must already be in progress in the still — board off the ground, body airborne. Standing-still skater in the still almost always gets "frozen" in the video. The fisheye distortion matters; V1 often defaults to rectilinear projection if you don't specify.
- Locked-off medium-wide of a small wooden table set for one, a half-full glass of red wine catching window light on the right, a paperback book lying open with pages mid-turn slightly out of focus, a single beeswax candle in a brass holder unlit, late afternoon warm sidelight, deep soft shadows, painterly chiaroscuro, Dutch still-life composition --ar 4:3 --v 7
  > Tune: the candle should be unlit in the still — V1 will animate the flicker on first lighting. Already-lit candle in the still often loses the flicker in the video because the model reads "this is the steady state."
