> The grade is the `--profile` — there is no anchor-pick step and no
> `--sref`. `--ar 16:9` because a decimal ratio is refused by this account
> (measured 2026-10-25: `--ar 1.85:1` → silent refusal, `--ar 16:9` → job
> accepted). The 1.85:1 framing lives in the composition, not the flag.
>
> `no text` suppresses signage. The bullets that depend on legible marks —
> redacted festival posters, the ELIGIBLE / NOT ELIGIBLE stamps, the MUSIC
> VIDEO shelf label, the price list — will come back with that text
> dissolved. Keep those frames for what they document; drop the suffix on a
> bullet only when the words matter more than the grade.
