- Hypnotic Bass Clarinet Meditation — Kai Djuric Dub. Subterranean bass clarinet ostinatos and key-click percussion, processed with granular synthesis and pitch-shifted delays, ride a 95 BPM minimal deep-house groove with a muted four-on-the-floor kick, crisp hi-hats and a cavernous sub-bass. Warm Rhodes chords and bowed vibraphone drones float over field recordings of underground rivers. Terry Riley's modal minimalism meets Colin Stetson's extended technique, but the pulse is pure late-night basement soul — meditative yet subtly propulsive, the kind of track that locks a room into a shared, trance-like sway. Soulful, jazzy and classy — deep house for the after-hours soul.
- Ethereal Soprano Saxophone Ascension — Soulful House Ascension. Soaring soprano saxophone melodies with crystalline glissandi, drenched in shimmering plate reverb and ping-pong delays, soar over a 122 BPM soulful house groove with a four-on-the-floor kick, live-feel percussion and a melodic fretless bassline. Harp arpeggios, glass harmonica textures, reversed cymbal swells and warm Rhodes chords fill the harmonic space, while Phrygian dominant ornamentation adds a Middle Eastern tinge to the spiritual jazz core. Alice Coltrane meets Floating Points, reshaped for the Sunday-morning dancefloor — uplifting, classy and dreamy, with an ecstatic intensity that builds and releases with the crowd.
- Gritty Baritone Saxophone Funk Sermon — Jackin' House Sermon. Raw baritone saxophone riffs — overblown honks, growls, slap-tonguing — recorded through saturated tape and vintage tube preamps, lock into a 124 BPM jackin' deep-house groove with a punchy kick, crisp snares and a rubbery electric bassline. Clavinet stabs, Hammond organ swells, wah-wah guitar chops and brass-section punches trade call-and-response with muted trumpet counterlines. Breakdowns are built on dub delay throws and tape-echo feedback before slamming back into the four-on-the-floor. Maceo Parker meets Kerri Chandler — streetwise, sweaty, preacher-at-the-pulpit energy for the peak-time floor.
- Whispered Cornet Nocturne — Deep House Nocturne. Intimate cornet whispers with Harmon-mute detail, close-mic'd breath and valve noise, float through lo-fi tape saturation and wow and flutter degradation over a 118 BPM deep-house pulse with a soft four-on-the-floor kick, brushed hats, upright bass and sparse Wurlitzer chords. Distant train whistles, rain-on-window field recordings and slowly evolving string pads create ghostly harmonic shadows, while tape-loop fragments repeat and decay into the groove. Chet Baker's whispered ballads meets the deep-house midnight hour — melancholic yet gently danceable, beautiful and classy without losing its 3 AM solitude.
- Cosmic Flute and EWI Odyssey — Afro-House Odyssey. Breathy concert flute melodies with airy overtones and flutter-tonguing, doubled by EWI on celestial pads with heavy pitch modulation and shimmer reverb, ride a 112 BPM afro-house groove with polyrhythmic percussion, talking-drum accents and a warm, rounded bassline. Kalimba patterns, marimba ostinatos and sun-drenched Rhodes chords glow beneath, while pentatonic call-and-response between flute and synthesized horn swells spirals through long delay trails. Yusef Lateef meets Black Coffee with Fela Kuti's rhythmic foundation — uplifting, expansive and deeply spiritual, a sunset celebration built for the dancefloor while remaining meditative at its core.
