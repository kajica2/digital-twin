Persona Social Pack.

One URL in. A whole social pack out. Point it at a page that describes creative personas: it finds every persona on that page and gives each one three hashtag tiers, YouTube metadata and four social posts — Instagram, TikTok, X and Facebook. The key stays in this tab.
